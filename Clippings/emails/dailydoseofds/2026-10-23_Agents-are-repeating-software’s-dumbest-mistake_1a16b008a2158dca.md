## [**Agents are repeating software’s dumbest mistake**](<http://go.copilotkit.ai/get-clif>)

The real moat in 2027 won’t be anything to do with the agent.

Instead, it’ll be by capturing full-stack trajectories.

Imagine two teams ship the same agent, on the same model, on the same day.

Your competitor ships it and moves on.

But you ship agents that learn from every session: where users stepped in, what they did by hand, what they keep asking for. Every week it picks up a few new skills, evals and datasets.

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tu0c!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb20319f5-65e6-4422-83e8-17c796bf523f_2752x1536.jpeg)

Six months later, you aren’t really competing on the same product anymore.

Because the advantage isn’t in the model but rather it’s in your users’ data.
