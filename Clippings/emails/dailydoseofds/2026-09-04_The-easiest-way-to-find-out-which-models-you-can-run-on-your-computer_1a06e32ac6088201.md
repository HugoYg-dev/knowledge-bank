## [**The easiest way to find out which models you can run on your computer**](<http://github.com/magnitudedev/magnitude>)

Just run:

`npm i -g @magnitudedev/cli  
magnitude setup`

It profiles your machine and ranks the models across:

  * Speed
  * Accuracy
  * Intelligence
  * Memory required
